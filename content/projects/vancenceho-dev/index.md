## About This Site

Apart from the purpose of serving as my portfolio website, this site also document some of my experiences as I navigate my journey and acts as a technical diary where I jot down my progress around this ever-changing tech world.

Just as how I was inspired in the very beginning, I hope to inspire others through my work and contribute to the public by using technology for the good of others. By actively contributing, I hope my work will help and inspire others to step out of their comfort zone to try something which that could help them achieve their goals.

This site itself is the first entry in the [Projects]({{< ref "projects" >}}) section - a personal portfolio built to showcase personal software and data engineering work. You can call it my personal tech dairy (Haha!). Lastly, while working on this site it helps me to learn the Hugo framework as well as the Blowfish theme, specifically, along the way.

Hopefully this is not a waste of time (lol!).
